- **`scripts/boards/refresh_bundles_6404.py --check` compares every committed board bundle to a fresh run** (Issue #10692). The flag is a `--dry-run` anchored to each board's committed `checked_at` that publishes nothing and exits 1 naming every drifted board and file, so a bundle whose per-rule violation counts no longer match what the current rules emit (Board 04's `pour_slot` 5 against a fresh 4) is caught, even when its archive is current, because `--check` re-runs the checker and compares per-rule counts with each committed check report (report bytes are not compared), by the new nightly bundle drift gate instead of by hand. It is not a per-PR check. A board whose committed receipt is `blocked` (Board 04, #10662/#10664) still fails `--check`, reported as `BLOCKED` rather than drift, so the nightly job stays red on `main` until that defect is repaired; a refused or blocked board never prints the "nothing to publish" line.
