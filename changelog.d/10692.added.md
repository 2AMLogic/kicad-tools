- **`scripts/boards/refresh_bundles_6404.py --check` compares every committed board bundle to a fresh run** (Issue #10692). The flag is a `--dry-run` anchored to each board's committed `checked_at` that publishes nothing and exits 1 naming every drifted board and file, so a bundle whose per-rule warning counts no longer match what the current rules emit (Board 04's `pour_slot` 5 against a fresh 4) is caught by the new nightly bundle drift gate instead of by hand. It is not a per-PR check.
