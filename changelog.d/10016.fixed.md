- **Declared current paths measure overlapping tracks at their physical width** (Issue #10016).
  A thin track extending beyond a wide trunk keeps its separate tail. Thin
  loops attached at one junction produce a located ampacity warning. Wholly
  buried tracks preserve wire and parallel reinforcement on a series feeder.
  `kct pcb prune` identifies retained loop copper and preserves DRC verification
  when its last pass finds no further edits.
