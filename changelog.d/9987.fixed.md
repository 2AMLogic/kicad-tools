- **`kct export` checks saved zone copper against the fabricator rules it ships** (Issue #9987). On Board 06, five 0.30 mm zone clearance rules took effect after routing. Export used to plot the earlier pours, leaving 499 native clearance errors on the archived board. Export now compares the saved copper with a native refill of a scratch copy under the final rules and preserves the PCB bytes when they agree. When a refill is needed, KiCad saves it before export plots the Gerbers, archives the project or records its hashes.
  A hidden `.<board>.kicad_pcb.zonefill` sidecar records the PCB and rule hashes so an unchanged export can reuse its result. Restoring or editing the PCB invalidates the record. Dry runs and bundles that contain neither Gerbers nor a project ZIP keep their existing behavior. A named `--stackup-id` stays bound to the bytes that ship.
