- **`kct export --strict-preflight` fails when a declared mounting hole is outside its drawing limits** (Issue #10476). The `assembly_mounting_hole_fit` check fails preflight on a confirmed mismatch: a nominal drill or pitch outside the declared limits, or an evidenced end of the fabricator's finished-hole interval beyond a diameter limit. Two 2.00 mm holes on 10.00 mm centres with JLCPCB's -0.08/+0.13 mm through-hole tolerance, checked against a 2.0 ±0.1 mm drawing, overrun the 2.10 mm maximum by 0.03 mm and fail. Projects that declare no `mounting_holes:` are not applicable and keep their result, as do lead-only declarations; an ordinary export records the findings in `manifest.json` and continues. Declare a documented order-specific interval in `finished_hole_guarantees:` when the fab has guaranteed a tighter one for those holes.
