- **`kct export` ships a supplementary purchasing list for accessories and
  off-board parts** (Issue #10431). A project declares `purchasing` rows in its
  `.kct` — a description, a whole-number quantity per board, optional linked
  designators and either a manufacturer and MPN or an explicit `pending` note —
  and the export writes them to `purchasing.json` with its SHA-256 and size in
  `manifest.json`, leaving the fabricator BOM and CPL byte-identical.
  `kct submit-jlcpcb` scales the frozen list by the board quantity into the
  plan and publishes it in the review page, whose "Purchasing list" section
  names each outstanding selection by description and linked designators. An
  invalid row fails the export and names the row; exporting again without the
  declaration withdraws the earlier list.
