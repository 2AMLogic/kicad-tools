- **Preflight can now fail and warn on through-hole lead fit** (Issue #10299). With verified lead dimensions declared in the `.kct` spec, a lead that cannot clear the finished plated hole — nominally, or at the fabricator's declared lower finished-hole tolerance — fails `kct export` and blocks strict exports. Boards whose plated through-hole pads lack verified lead dimensions, or whose finished-hole tolerance is not declared, get an *unassessed* warning naming the pads; previously these boards passed preflight silently.
