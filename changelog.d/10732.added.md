- **`kct fix-drc --only acute-branch` re-routes a tap that meets the side of a
  same-net carrier at an acute angle** (Issue #10732). The tap keeps its
  anchor, width, net and layer and meets the carrier at a right angle: one
  straight leg when the anchor lies over the carrier, else a dogleg whose
  landing is read from the carrier (inside its extent by half the tap's width,
  a tap width from its other landings). The carrier and its other taps are
  untouched and no coordinate is hand-picked. Each edit is gated by
  `sharp_trace_corner`'s own test at every new vertex and by the
  `redundant_copper` rule's clearance, mask and slit gates, then re-checked on
  the saved, refilled board and put back when it leaves a new error, a new
  `redundant_copper` warning or a new `track_dangling` / `via_dangling` /
  `physical_copper_gap` warning (any new warning with `--no-new-warnings`). A
  tap whose right-angle entry would turn its own feeding tracks into a
  `redundant_copper` staircase or detour is left as found and reported with
  that reason. On Board 03's published copper, after the redundant-copper
  cleanup, it takes the board from 22 such junctions to 3 on the pinned
  toolchain.
