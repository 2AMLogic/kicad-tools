- **`kct export` preflight checks through-hole lead fit against the finished hole** (Issue #10299). A `.kct` `bom_entries` entry can now declare verified maximum lead dimensions for its part assignment (`lead:` with a round `diameter_mm` or rectangular `width_mm`/`thickness_mm` and a mandatory `source`), plus per-pad-number overrides via `pad_leads:` for mixed-lead packages; `requirements.manufacturing.finished_hole_tolerance_mm` (with its own source) carries the fabricator's lower finished-hole tolerance. The new `assembly_lead_fit` check reports, for every plated through-hole pad, the MPN, reference, pad, hole size, lead dimensions and source, the nominal diametral margin, and the margin at minimum finished hole. A negative margin is a confirmed interference and fails preflight; missing lead data, slot holes and an unknown tolerance are reported as unassessed.
