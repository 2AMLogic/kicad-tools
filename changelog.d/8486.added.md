- **Per-call route tracing now identifies the connection, target and fence path behind each search**
  (Issue #8486). Records drained from the C++ pathfinder's
  `enable_per_call_timing` instrumentation carry, in addition to the existing
  net/elapsed/budget/success fields, the chosen pad pair, the legal goal
  bounds and layers, whether every legal goal cell was trace-fence covered,
  the escape-fence decision (`applied`, `skipped-covered` or `none`), each
  native search's duration and outcome, and any Python-fallback handoff
  reason. A saved trace can now distinguish a drained fenced search from a
  pre-empted one and name the exact destination considered — the data the
  reported board's 942.72 s of duplicate fence-retry timeouts could not be
  attributed with. Purely observational: the produced copper is identical
  with tracing off and on.
