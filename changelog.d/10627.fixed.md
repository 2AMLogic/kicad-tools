- **Pour completion writes net-class width, not the board width** (Issue
  #10627). `PourLinkCloser` closed `+5V`/`GND` pour links with pad-to-via
  welds and routed links at the 0.20 mm board width although their `Power`
  class is 0.50 mm, so `netclass_track_width_mismatch` fired on every new
  segment. Candidate clearance, weld neighbour reach and the written copper
  now use each pour net's class `trace_width` (mixed classes are welded one
  width group at a time). A declared `neck_trace_width` does not narrow
  completion copper, and a link or weld that cannot fit at full width stays
  open or skipped with a diagnostic instead of emitting an undersized segment.
  Weld stubs also keep the fab-view same-net trace slit band, so full-width
  stubs no longer land 0.05-0.10 mm apart (`physical_copper_gap`).
