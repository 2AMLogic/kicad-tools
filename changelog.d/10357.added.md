- **`kct export --via-land-overlap` measures plated via drills against the shipped
  PCBA artwork** (Issue #10357). The opt-in reads the final Gerber archive after
  assembly generation and reports every via drill that #10356 matched to a routed
  via whose exported disk shares area with the solder-mask opening, stencil
  opening and an SMD land on one face, including edge overlaps whose drill center
  lies outside the opening. Each observation names its face, reference, pad,
  drill coordinate in the shipped Excellon frame, exported diameter and declared
  process disposition: `filled_capped` under the bundle's declared via-in-pad
  process, `exposed_thermal` on a pad carrying KiCad's heatsink pad property, or
  `undeclared`. A physical hole is counted once however many faces observe it.
  Coverage is recorded per face with expected and matched via counts; a missing,
  unsupported or unregistered input, an unmatched via or an opening shared by two
  lands is named as a reason and the result is never presented as a complete
  zero. The `via_land_overlap` result is appended to the preflight list in
  `--format json` and `manifest.json`; a standard export is unchanged.
