- **Placed footprints carry their embedded zones to the board** (Issue #8958).
  `kct create-pcb` and `PCB.add_footprint_from_file()` left a footprint's zones
  (an RF module's antenna keepout) at the library's own coordinates near the
  sheet origin, and KiCad's DRC reported `lib_footprint_mismatch`. The outline
  is now placed with the footprint's position, rotation and side. A back-side
  placement also mirrors inner copper layers through the board's stack
  (`In1.Cu` to `In2.Cu` on four layers), as KiCad does.
