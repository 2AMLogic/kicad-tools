- **`kct analyze reference-coverage` reports `not_applicable` on a layer a net only passes through on a via** (Issue #10515). A known reference side with no routed copper on that layer previously reported `coverage: "none"`, the same value as a trace routed entirely over a void, so consumers hunting unreferenced copper had to also consult `length_mm`. `"none"` now means positive-length copper with no reference, while a side with missing saved fill still reports `"unknown"`.
