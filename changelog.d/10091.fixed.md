- **A refused named-stackup silk repair no longer leaves generated DRC sidecars behind** (Issue #10091). When `kct export --stackup-id` stops because the requested silk legalisation could not run, the `.kicad_pro` and `.kicad_dru` beside the board are left exactly as they were found: absent siblings stay absent, authored files keep their bytes, and an editor save that lands while the export runs is preserved. The error text, exit status and package refusal are unchanged, and ordinary preflight failures still emit the rules as before.
