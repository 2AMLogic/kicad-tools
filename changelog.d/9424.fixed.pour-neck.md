- **Pour completion can use a net class's declared pad-escape width** (Issue
  #9424, follow-up to #10627). The router tries full-width copper first. If
  it cannot fit, a narrower `neck_trace_width` permits a short escape from
  the pad to a via or nearby same-net copper. The escape must satisfy the
  net-class agreement check and is recorded in the router's notes. If the
  class supplies no narrower width and no full-width path fits, the router
  reports the open connection.
