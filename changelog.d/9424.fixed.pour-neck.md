- **Pour completion necks a weld the class width cannot place** (Issue #9424,
  follow-up to #10627). `PourLinkCloser` still welds at the class
  `trace_width` first; a pad-to-via stub no strategy can place at that width
  -- a 0.50 mm stub cannot leave a 0.40 mm pin on 0.65 mm pitch with 0.20 mm
  clearance, which left board 05's unattended route at 44/46 nets -- is now
  retried as a short pad escape at the class's `neck_trace_width`, or at the
  board width when the class declares none. The retry writes only a straight
  leg or one 45-degree dogleg landing inside the `netclass_agreement` rule's
  pad-escape reach. Where the stitcher's fixed via sites cannot reach, the
  link router searches the eight 0/45/90-degree rays from the pad centre for
  the nearest same-net landing and writes that one leg. Every necked weld is
  named in the closer notes. Routed links that need more than one pad escape
  are unchanged: a link that cannot fit at full width still stays open.
