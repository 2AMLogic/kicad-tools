- **`kct check` now checks copper arc tracks against the minimum trace width** (Issue #10173). The `dimension_trace_width` rule previously read only straight segments; arcs narrower than the manufacturer's `min_trace_width_mm` now raise an error, and outer-layer arcs on the JLCPCB 4-layer 1 oz stackup narrower than 0.1524 mm raise the new warning. Existing boards with sub-minimum arc tracks can newly fail.
