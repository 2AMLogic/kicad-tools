- **Board 07's publisher now writes public release summaries that describe the shipped
  package** (Issue #9955). `boards/07-matchgroup-test/output/build.json` and
  `output/status.json` used to carry the routing-stage builder's copy verbatim: `pcb` and
  `evidence` pointed at staging paths that do not resolve from `output/`, and the files
  still said manufacturing was incomplete and an export was required after the gated
  release had passed its post-export checks. The publisher derives both summaries from the
  staging build's own summary, keeping the recorded routing facts (`parts`, `bound_pads`,
  `routing_method`, `hardware_tested`, `routed`) while pointing at the published routed
  PCB, `readiness/validation.json`, the `readiness.json` receipt and `manufacturing.zip`,
  and marking the package complete. Stale summaries on an otherwise-current bundle are
  repaired through the same gated transaction: a dry run names the two files, a successful
  publish rewrites exactly them, a repeat proposes zero changes, and any refused
  publication (mismatched rules or a not-ready receipt) leaves the committed release
  byte-for-byte intact. The staging builder's own summaries and the design-preflight
  `validation.json` keep describing their own scope.
