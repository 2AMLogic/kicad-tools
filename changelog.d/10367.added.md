- **`kct fix-drc --only copper-gap` repairs same-net trace shelves at routed branches** (Issue #10367). A
  `physical_copper_gap` between a trunk segment and a short same-net staircase that leaves one of its ends is
  now fixed by replacing that branch chain with a straight or 45-degree-plus-H/V shortcut that keeps the trunk
  node, the branch's far end and its first and last widths. Interior pads, vias, taps, layer changes and
  reinforcement endpoints protect the chain; the rewrite is declined (`would_violate_clearance`,
  `gap_not_cleared`) unless the new copper clears every neighbour and the slit is gone. `--dry-run` lists the
  shelf and both end nodes; locked segments are never touched.
