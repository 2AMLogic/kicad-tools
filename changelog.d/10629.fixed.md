- **Analysis pad loader no longer reads a non-plated hole as plated** (Issue #10629). `load_pads_for_analysis` now decides `through_hole` from the parsed pad type (as `load_pcb_for_routing` does) and resolves the copper layer from the pad's authored `(layers ...)`, so `LatticePathfinder.from_board` applies the NPTH `hole_clearance` keep-out, inner-layer SMD pads keep their layer, and a quoted `thru_hole` / `B.Cu` in a pad property no longer flips either field. `MeshPathfinder` treats any drilled pad (including NPTH) as an obstacle on every copper layer.
