- **The reference settle pass in `legalize_silk` cannot loop indefinitely** (Issue #9957). `SilkRefPlacer.plan(settle=True)` runs at most 32 rounds, and references still waiting for a retry round when it stops are listed in the new `PlaceSilkRefsResult.unsettled_refs` instead of being dropped. The suspected mutual displacement could not be reproduced: a candidate is always checked against the other references' current positions, so a placed reference only ever moves to a larger courtyard margin. Boards that settle within the cap, which includes every board in this repository, plan exactly as before.
