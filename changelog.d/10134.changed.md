- **`kct detect-mistakes` reports decoupling capacitors by their designated supply pin** (Issue #10134). The check reads the project schematic and uses the `KCT_Decouples` override or drawn association (#10140). When the schematic is unavailable it falls back to the 5 mm nearest-pad rule. Warnings identify distant designated capacitors and the owner of nearby capacitors on the same rail. `--format json` lists every IC power pin in `decoupling_inventory`, including clean and empty pins, with each capacitor’s value, PCB distance and association source.
