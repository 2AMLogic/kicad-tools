- **`kct route-link` keeps tracks out of non-plated mounting holes** (Issue #10393). A straight link no longer crosses the opening of an `np_thru_hole` pad: the drill now demands a `hole_clearance` (default 0.25 mm, KiCad's `min_hole_clearance`) from tracks and vias on every copper layer, and annular copper on the pad is treated as unnetted foreign copper. The link detours instead of committing a track that native DRC rolls back.
