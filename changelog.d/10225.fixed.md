- **`kct placement optimize` places designated bypass capacitors at their IC pins** (Issue #10225). Ordinary and routing-aware placement honor schematic associations and `KCT_Decouples`. Both modes preserve fixed footprints and locks inherited from KiCad groups. Before saving, they check that the capacitor and its target pin both have copper on the designated side, meet the distance limit and follow capacitance order. Set the maximum distance with `--decoupling-radius MM` (default 6 mm).
