- **A `purchasing` row's terminal map must stay within its `refs`** (Issue
  #10582). A project whose `terminals[].ref` names a footprint missing from the
  row's non-empty `refs` now fails to load. Add that designator to `refs` (a
  part bridging two landings lists both, at `quantity: 1`) or correct the
  terminal's `ref`. Rows with omitted or empty `refs`, and rows without a
  terminal map, are unaffected.
