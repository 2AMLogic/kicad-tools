- **`kct fleet status` no longer reports a board ship-ready when its
  readiness receipt says `blocked`** (Issue #10662). The survey read
  `output/readiness.json` only for its `known_issues`, so Board 04 -- whose
  producer had re-pinned `ready` on a board `kct readiness --verify` blocked
  -- still showed `YES*`. An explicit `blocked` receipt is now a
  `readiness blocked: ...` blocker; a missing, unreadable or `unverified`
  report still adds nothing. Board 04's committed receipt is published
  `blocked`: its #6269 paid-drill exception is re-pinned to the current via
  identities (69 exact), and the ten same-net `mask_to_copper` errors the
  public check reports stay unaccepted (#10664).
