- **`kct route` stops re-searching stalled nets at the full per-net cap when only copper the net
  cannot reach has moved** (Issue #8486). The November 2026 residual after #9904: the
  known-failure back-off keyed its blocking-copper witness on every foreign shape on the board,
  so any remote reroute reset the streak and the reported narrow nets kept their full ~30 s
  budget every iteration. The witness is now scoped to the net's reachable search space: its
  terminal window plus the free space a coarse fill can walk to from its terminals across
  layer-agnostic free cells. Copper sealed behind walls the search can never cross no longer
  restarts the back-off, while removing or moving copper that bounds the reachable space -- or
  shifting it near the terminals -- still restores the full budget immediately, and the
  three-iteration full re-probe is unchanged. Back-off therefore requires a repeatable
  blocking-copper signature: a shape that legitimately moves inside the scope still resets it,
  and restored partial copper is only re-landed when it stays legal on the current board.
