- **`kct check` evaluates net-class patterns containing `+`, `.` and parentheses** (Issue #10122). Supply names such as `+3.3V` and generated names such as `Net-(U5-CAPP)` now resolve through the native-verified matcher instead of reporting `netclass_clearance_unsupported` and skipping the copper check. The parenthesized spelling also covers KiCad's unparenthesized form (`Net-U5-CAPP`), while similarly named nets (`+3x3V`, `Net-(U5-CAPX)`) correctly keep the default class. Syntax outside the verified subset — alternation, brackets, braces, backslashes, quantified or nested groups — still reports the explicit unsupported result.
