- **`kct pcb reinforce --clear` no longer saves the board before failing on an unreadable sidecar** (Issue #10419). When a clear would remove anchors or a parallel copy and the destination `<board>.reinforcement.json` (next to the `-o` output when given) is not a version-1 document with a `wires` list, the command exits 1 naming that path and the reason before it reports or writes anything: the PCB, the unreadable sidecar and any project sidecars keep their exact bytes, and a fresh `-o` output stays absent. An unreadable sidecar at the source alone still falls back to size-based anchor discovery (#8747) when the destination is usable, and `--dry-run`, a missing sidecar and a clear with nothing to remove are unchanged.
