- **Verified mask repairs preserve routed trace angles** (Issue #9939). When moving a junction would skew an existing horizontal, vertical or 45° track, `kct fix-drc --only mask-copper` tries two connected, angle-aligned alternatives. It checks the new copper against nearby contacts, the mask openings and the project's clearance rules, including traces that moved with the junction. A rejected repair restores its copper and group membership. The final saved board is checked for newly off-angle tracks after any zone refill.
