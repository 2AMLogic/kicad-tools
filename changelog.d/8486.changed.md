- **Per-call route traces now say how each Python fallback relates to earlier ones** (Issue #8486). With per-call timing on, the `python_fallback` record carries `request` (`first` / `repeat` / `changed_request`), for a repeat whether the grid the earlier search read is `unchanged` or `changed` and how that search ended (`prior_outcome`), what the fallback did (`action`: `ran` or the guard that skipped it) and, when it ran, whether it `drained` its open set, hit its wall-clock `deadline`, `stopped` otherwise or `found` a route, with its seconds and budget. Observational only: routing decisions and the untraced fast path are unchanged.
