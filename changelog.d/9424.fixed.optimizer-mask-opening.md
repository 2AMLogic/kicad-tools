- **`kct route` checks trace shortcuts against the mask openings of their own pads** (Issue #9424). With a manufacturer mask policy, the trace optimizer declines a rewrite that adds same-net copper beside a pad's opening: a bend whose rounded corner comes within the mask-to-copper clearance of a pad it does not enter, or copper over an opening that is not one clean entry. A rewrite is kept when the unoptimised chain already carries as many. On Board 05's unattended seed-42 route this clears four `mask_to_copper` errors.
