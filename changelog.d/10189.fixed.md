- **`kct route` and `kct optimize-traces` honour project netclass `track_width` on re-runs** (Issue #10189). `load_pcb_for_routing` now feeds non-`Default` project netclass `track_width` declarations (resolved via `authored_netclass_widths` across both `netclass_assignments` and `netclass_patterns`) into per-net routing widths ahead of the router's name-classified defaults while keeping explicit `--trace-width`, `--net-class-map` and board-wide `Default` precedence intact. Re-running `kct route` after editing a class's `track_width` in `.kicad_pro` re-routes only the nets whose existing copper disagrees with the updated width, leaving other classes' copper unchanged; `kct optimize-traces` likewise updates rewritten segments to their project class `track_width` when collision-safe. A short wide join no longer hides an old-width trunk. Curved tracks retain their shape during optimization, and a whole-net reroute with `--nets` replaces the selected net's old arcs. Direct and inherited KiCad locks protect routed copper during either operation.
