- **`kct export` carries a purchased part's terminal map into the assembly
  handoff** (Issue #10514). A selected `purchasing` row in the `.kct` may add
  `terminals`, each pairing one of the part's own terminal numbers with a board
  landing (`ref`, `pad`, `net`) or marking it `unused` — for a panel part wired
  to a landing whose pad numbers differ from its terminals. The export checks
  every mapped landing against the PCB and refuses, naming the row, the terminal
  and the landing, when the reference or pad is missing or the pad carries
  another net; a terminal assigned twice, or both mapped and unused, is refused
  too. The validated map travels in `purchasing.json` and the review page shows
  it as a "Terminal map" table that separates the project's terminal
  declarations from the board lookups, leaving the fabricator BOM and CPL
  byte-identical.
