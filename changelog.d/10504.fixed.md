- **`kct route-link` keeps the board's own hole clearance from non-plated holes** (Issue #10504). The gap a link kept from a bare drill was a fixed 0.25 mm (#10393); it is now at least the `hole_clearance` the board resolves -- `.kicad_pro` `min_hole_clearance`, or a `.kicad_dru` `hole_clearance` rule the NPTH pad matches -- whether or not the board declares a copper rule, so a link no longer commits a track that native DRC rolls back. A larger `hole_clearance` or per-net floor still wins. `--probe` now reports a blocking drill as kind `hole`; a pad is still `pad`.
