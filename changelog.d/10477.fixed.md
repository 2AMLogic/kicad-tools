- **A second solder-mask opening over a buttress-wire anchor no longer reads `unverified_registration`** (Issue #10477). The `reinforcement_clearance` entry of `kct export`'s preflight report accepted an anchor only when its copper flash was concentric with *every* plotted mask opening standing on it, so where mask openings merge near an anchor -- adjacent pad expansions bridged into one region, or an anchor inside a larger mask cutout -- a genuinely open, registered anchor was reported incomplete instead of its two distances. One concentric opening now corroborates the anchor, and the other openings still add their mask area to both minima. A mask shifted by less than the opening's radius, with or without a second opening, and an anchor without its copper flash are still reported `unverified_registration`, and valid measurements on the other face are kept.
