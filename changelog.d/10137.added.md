- **`kct check --prove-pour-stubs` identifies pad branches that duplicate a pour connection** (Issue #10137). The advisory `redundant_copper_pour_stub` warning names the pad and track, including its terminal via when present. It appears only after a scratch refill shows that the pour still connects the pad and the other terminal connections survive. The proof runs one kicad-cli refill per candidate branch, so it is opt-in and the default `kct check` cost is unchanged.
