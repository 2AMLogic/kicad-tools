- **`kicad_tools.analysis.analog_pin_range` checks simulated pin voltages
  against supply-state limits** (Issue #10527). Run
  `python -m kicad_tools.analysis.analog_pin_range board.kicad_sch pin-ranges.json`
  with a reviewed sidecar and externally generated transient samples. Each pin
  assertion binds a reference, pin and schematic net to a trace and to the
  limits for one `powered` or `unpowered` state; the report gives the signed
  extrema over the named window, every violated side with its excursion in
  volts, and a pass, fail or unassessed outcome. Results without modeled input
  protection are labelled `unclamped demand`, and a missing pin binding,
  state-specific limit, rail evidence, model assumption or sound samples is
  unassessed with a reason instead of passing. Nothing is simulated and no
  rating is looked up. See `docs/guides/analog-pin-ranges.md`.
