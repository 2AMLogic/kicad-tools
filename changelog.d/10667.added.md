- **`kct fix-drc --only redundant-copper` clears the trace-quality findings
  `kct readiness` refuses, without trading them for new ones** (Issue #10667).
  It removes a track hidden under a longer collinear one without splitting
  the covering track (so no tap becomes an acute junction), and replaces a
  staircase or detour with the `redundant_copper` rule's own fewer-bend path.
  Each edit is re-checked on the saved, refilled board -- `kct check`'s rule
  set with the mask assessment and native DRC -- and put back when it leaves
  a new error or a new `sharp_trace_corner` / `track_dangling` /
  `physical_copper_gap` warning (any new warning with `--no-new-warnings`);
  the chain's next legal path is then planned in its place. On the repaired
  Board 03 it takes the board from 84 such warnings to 0 with no new
  `sharp_trace_corner`, where `kct pcb prune` and `kct optimize-traces` each
  left dozens behind and added 16 and 10 sharp corners. `kct readiness` now
  runs it as the last step of its copper cleanup, after `dedupe`, `prune` and
  `optimize-traces`, and keeps each of those two passes only when it adds no
  `sharp_trace_corner` or `redundant_copper` finding (the board before the
  pass is restored and the step recorded as `not-applied` otherwise, since
  their native-DRC verification cannot see an explicit acute junction). On a
  board with none of its findings the new step is recorded as `not-needed`.
