- **`kct fix-drc --only mask-copper` pulls short pad escapes into the pad** (Issue #9891). A same-net entry whose end stops at or just outside its own pad's edge — the shape the tapered pad escapes leave — is now dragged to the pad centre, making it the single straight entry `mask_to_copper`'s `within_own_pad` class excuses; entries that would cross the pad edge at over 45 degrees are still declined. Undo no longer corrupts a segment whose other end already sits on the move target, and a joined end is never moved twice. `relocate_pad_edge_vias` gained a `ring_dam_mm` mode that judges a via by its copper ring against the pad aperture (the `mask_to_copper` view) instead of its drill. Both repairs honor KiCad copper locks and local pad and footprint clearance overrides. They preserve existing connections on the affected net, including when other pads on that net are already disconnected. Track pull-ins also check the saved copper with native DRC before writing the result.
