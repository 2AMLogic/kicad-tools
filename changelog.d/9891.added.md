- **`kct fix-drc --only mask-copper` pulls short pad escapes into the pad** (Issue #9891). A same-net entry whose end stops at or just outside its own pad's edge — the shape the tapered pad escapes leave — is now dragged to the pad centre, making it the single straight entry `mask_to_copper`'s `within_own_pad` class excuses; entries that would cross the pad edge at over 45 degrees are still declined. Undo no longer corrupts a segment whose other end already sits on the move target, and a joined end is never moved twice. `relocate_pad_edge_vias` gained a `ring_dam_mm` mode that judges a via by its copper ring against the pad aperture (the `mask_to_copper` view) instead of its drill. Both repairs honor KiCad copper locks and preserve existing connections on the affected net, including when other pads on the net are already disconnected. Track pull-ins honor pad and footprint clearance overrides and check the saved copper with native DRC before writing the result.
