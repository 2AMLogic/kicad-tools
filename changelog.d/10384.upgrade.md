- **`silk_over_copper` reports differently around untented vias** (Issue #10384). On a board that declares a non-zero `pad_to_mask_clearance`, silk inside the clearance ring around an untented via is now reported, as it already was beside a pad: move the silk clear of the opening or tent the via. On a board that declares `0`, an untented via's opening is now its bare copper, so silk that only the profile's margin had reached is no longer reported.
