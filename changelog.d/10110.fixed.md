- **`kct route`'s partial-net probe no longer launches native fabrication checks** (Issue #10110). The rescue/completion loop's connectivity probe ran a full `kct check --mfr ...` child, so on a machine with KiCad installed it also exported Gerbers and ran pcbnew for mask geometry -- work whose findings the probe never read, and enough native traffic to push the partial-route golden past its 60-second limit. The probe now runs `kct check --only connectivity` and reports the same partial/unrouted nets with no kicad-cli children; strict unreadable-report handling, the manufacturer argument and the separate candidate-acceptance and DRC gates are unchanged.
