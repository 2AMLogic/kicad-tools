- **A tripled layer change no longer makes a whole net `ambiguous`**
  (Issue #9725). Two or more same-net vias in a row, each joined directly to
  the next by a bar on both of the same two layers at a pitch of at most twice
  the largest pad diameter in the row, now resolve as one layer change whose
  capacity is the sum of the barrels. Dividing either straight bar into
  collinear track segments preserves that rating and the reinforcement anchors,
  including overlapping pieces and changes in track width. The audit uses the
  actual width of each piece.
  A barrel missing either bar, a wider pitch, an
  unmodeled via or pad touching a bar, or copper doubled on both layers through
  the row still stays `ambiguous`. A bypass that touches a feeder ahead of the
  row also stays ambiguous, including when the feeder is split at a barrel.
  After a genuine via row, a return that touches only the edge of a downstream
  lane is reported with both physical branches and receives no anchor on the shunted lane.
  `kct pcb reinforce` can anchor a lane outside a loop when every physical
  route between the declared pads traverses the whole segment. A return
  touching the edge of a track or joining midway leaves that segment
  unanchored and reported as unhandled. The `ambiguous` witness names both
  branches of the loop (`[loop branches: ...]` and `loop_branches` in JSON).
