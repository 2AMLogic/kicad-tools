- **`kct route` stops re-searching stalled unsealed nets at the full per-net cap every iteration** (Issue #8486).
  On a zero-overflow-stalled board the negotiated loop searched each still-unrouted net twice per
  iteration — the zero-overflow recovery and the targeted-ripup fallback — and neither search fed the
  #8486 known-failure back-off, so the reported narrow signal nets kept the full ~30 s cap each time
  and only one ever backed off. Both searches now back off while the foreign net/layer pairs present
  at the first failure remain in the corridor. Extra copper can come and go. Removing an original pair
  restores the full budget on the next search, including a retry immediately after a full rip-up.
  Every three iterations a full retry also catches changes within a net and layer. Failed nets stay
  in the failed-nets report and the per-iteration known-fail seconds line now counts these searches.
