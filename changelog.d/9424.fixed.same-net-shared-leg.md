- **`kct route` keeps a shared same-net escape leg once** (Issue #9424). When
  several connections of one net leave the same pad, the trace optimizer's
  pad-entry straightening and via minimisation lay their copies of the escape
  leg on one line, which `kct check` reports as `redundant_copper_covered`,
  and a 45-degree branch off the shared trunk is an acute junction
  (`sharp_trace_corner`). After the optimize pass the router now drops each
  copy that lies wholly on a longer, no-narrower same-net leg, cuts a dead tip
  that a drop exposes back to its via, and lets an acute branch that ends in
  its own via enter it perpendicular to the trunk when the clearance gates
  accept the new leg. On board 05's unattended route this takes the non-mask
  `kct check` from 9 warnings to 0 on seeds 42, 7 and 1337, and the full
  JLCPCB check from 12 to 11 `mask_to_copper` errors.
