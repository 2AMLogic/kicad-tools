- **`kct route` stops re-searching partially connected nets at the full per-net cap** (Issue #8486).
  The November 2026 follow-up to the known-failure back-off: a net that enters a rip-up iteration with
  only some pads connected was outside the failure record entirely — its nonempty partial return
  cleared the streak, so every iteration re-searched it at the full cap and a worse or empty re-search
  silently discarded its partial copper (one reported net lost two pad pairs that way). Partially
  connected nets now enter the same known-failure record as unrouted ones, and a re-search only counts
  as progress when it connects strictly more physical pad pairs. A no-progress result keeps the streak
  (so the existing budget back-off, its periodic full re-probe and the immediate full attempt after a
  blocking-copper change all apply) and the kept copper is the better of the entry state and the new
  result, so repeated retries can never strand already-connected pads. The deterministic
  (no-wall-clock) arm is untouched.
