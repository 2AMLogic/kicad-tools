- **`kct pcb reinforce --voltage-map` no longer accepts anchors that `kct creepage` fails** (Issue #10015). The pairwise table is built without the `--hv-threshold` floor, so a sub-threshold pair (e.g. 0 V vs 15 V under the default 30 V) now carries its standard-derived clearance into anchor placement, end-offset search and `--max-end-shift`. `--hv-threshold` is still accepted but no longer exempts pairs involving the reinforced net.
