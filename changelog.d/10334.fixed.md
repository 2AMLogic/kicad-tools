- **Hierarchical net names no longer change when an unrelated sheet reuses a label** (Issue #10334). In a multi-sheet design, `Schematic.extract_netlist(hierarchical=True)` and `build_netlist_from_schematic()` now always name a local or hierarchical-label net with its owning sheet-instance path (`/NAME` at the root, `/Sheet/NAME` in a child), instead of qualifying only when two sheets happened to share the text. Global and power names, single-sheet schematics and automatically generated names are unchanged.
