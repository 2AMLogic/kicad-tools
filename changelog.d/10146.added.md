- **`kct optimize-placement` keeps schematic groups together and in schematic
  order** (Issue #10146). Two new soft cost terms, on by default at weight
  `1.0`, group footprints by the schematic sheet their symbols sit on: a
  per-sheet cohesion spread (`--weights '{"group_cohesion": …}'`) and a
  within-sheet order penalty that flags same-prefix parts placed against
  their annotation order (`--weights '{"group_order": …}'`). The sheet map is
  resolved from the schematic next to the PCB; single-sheet and
  schematic-less boards, sheets with fewer than three members, and zeroed
  weights all keep the previous objective unchanged.
