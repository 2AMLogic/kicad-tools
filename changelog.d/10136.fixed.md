- **`kct route --stitch-power-planes` no longer adds a stub and via to SMD pads already bonded to the plane by filled copper** (Issue #10136). A pad is skipped only when current, layer-specific fill (solid or thermal spokes) touches it and shares a copper-contact component with the net's plane; outline-only zones, stale fills, isolated surface islands and disabled pad connections still get a legal stitch, and forced native-open repairs still win. Skipped pads and the reason are listed under `pour_bonded` in the stitch result.
