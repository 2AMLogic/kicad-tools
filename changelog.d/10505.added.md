- **`kct export` preflight checks two-terminal SMD land patterns against MPN-specific drawings** (Issue #10505). A `.kct` `bom_entries` entry can now declare a source-attributed `land_pattern` (`pad_shape`, `pad_depth_mm`, `pad_width_mm`, `pitch_mm` and/or `outer_span_mm`, and mandatory manufacturer drawing/page `source`). The new `assembly_smd_land_pattern` check reads the placed `.kicad_pcb` without modifying it, undoes placement rotation and front-to-back mirroring, and compares both copper pads' shape, terminal-axis depth, transverse width, center pitch, and outer copper span within `+/-0.001 mm`. Matching by footprint or package name alone never counts as verification; missing MPNs, missing or source-less declarations, and unusable pad geometry are reported as unassessed with explicit reasons.
