- **`kct fix-drc --only mask-copper` now repairs same-net pass-through copper beside a pad's mask opening** (Issue #9896). A track or via that passes beside its own pad's opening closer than the 0.09 mm policy clearance is translated away from the opening until it clears the clearance plus a margin: a segment moves with every same-net end joined to it, a via drags its anchored track ends, and one grouped edit per conductor must clear every opening it has findings against. Every move is gated against other-net copper, the shared drill/copper clearance engine, nearby mask openings and pads the moved ends terminate in, and `fix-drc`'s re-check still reverts any move that does not clear its finding or creates a new one. On board 03's USB joystick one pass repairs 21 of the 45 beside-pad findings (both witnesses resolved: the SW2 via moves 0.117 mm clear, the J2 track stays with a junction-at-via reason), leaves connectivity at 27/27 nets and introduces no new DRC errors; the rest keep concrete reasons (`junction_at_moved_end`, `would_violate_clearance` with the measured gap, `infeasible` between facing openings).
