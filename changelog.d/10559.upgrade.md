- **A hand-edited wire-list sidecar with a malformed coordinate now refuses more commands** (Issue #10559).
  A pass that used to get past another net's damaged record -- `--clear`, `--replace` or a `--strategy parallel`
  re-run on a different net, or an ordinary pass whose merge only needed the leg ends -- now exits 1 and names
  the record and field, such as `<sidecar>: wires[1].anchors[0] is a string, expected an object with numeric 'x'
  and 'y'`. Nothing is written until the named record is repaired or removed; the read-only consumers
  (`kct check`, export, `--list-wires --format json`) are unchanged.
