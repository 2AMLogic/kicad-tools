- **`kct detect-mistakes` counts bypass capacitors per IC power pin** (Issue #10134, part of).
  New `DecouplingCountCheck` (category `decoupling`, advisory) assigns each
  decoupling cap to the nearest power pad on its net within 5 mm and flags a
  pin with more than one capacitor of the same value, or with none nearby on a
  rail that is decoupled elsewhere. Findings list the capacitors, values and
  distances; the full per-pin inventory, including clean and empty pins, is
  available from `DecouplingCountCheck.pin_inventory()`. The association is
  PCB-geometric; schematic association (#10131) and the `schematic/blocks`
  count are not part of this change.
