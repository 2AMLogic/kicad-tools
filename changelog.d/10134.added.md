- **`kct detect-mistakes` counts bypass capacitors per IC power pin** (Issue #10134, part of).
  New `DecouplingCountCheck` (category `decoupling`, advisory) assigns each
  decoupling cap to the nearest power pad on its net within 5 mm and flags a
  pin with more than one capacitor of the same value, or with no separately
  assigned capacitor on a rail that is decoupled elsewhere (distinguishing a
  nearby capacitor assigned to another pin from one outside the 5 mm radius).
  Findings list the capacitors, values, distances and pin assignments; the full
  per-pin inventory, including clean and empty pins, is available from
  `DecouplingCountCheck.pin_inventory()`. The association is PCB-geometric;
  schematic association (#10131) and the `schematic/blocks` count are not part
  of this change.
