- **`kct optimize-placement` pairs bypass caps by their schematic designation** (Issue #10142). When a `.kicad_sch` sits beside the board, each decoupling cap goes to the pin it serves in the schematic (including a `KCT_Decouples` override) instead of the nearest pin; unassociated caps keep the distance-greedy pairing. The snap pass now snaps the smallest cap on a pin first and never leaves a larger cap nearer the pin than a smaller one. New `--decoupling-radius MM` (default 6.0) sets how far from its pin a cap may sit. Movable designated caps can flip to the IC side. The command checks the saved pad positions and sides before publishing the result.
