- **`kct:circuit-review` grades explicit datasheet contradictions at least `warning`, and `kct:tapeout` gates on the review's open decisions** (Issue #10135). A component value that contradicts an explicit datasheet statement (pin-table text, "must"/"should" wording, a stated range) is tagged `[basis: explicit]` and can no longer be graded `note`; a difference from a typical-application figure only is `[basis: typical]` and may stay `note`. Every `blocker`/`warning` finding now lands in an `### Open decisions` table at the top of `CIRCUIT_REVIEW.md` that the owner must resolve (`fixed` / `accepted: <reason>`) — the review never fills in a decision itself.
