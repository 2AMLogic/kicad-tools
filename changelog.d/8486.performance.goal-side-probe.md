- **`kct route` proves a repeatedly timed-out connection from its goal side instead of re-running the full-budget search** (Issue #8486). A pad-pair request
  whose C++ search ended on the per-net wall-clock deadline used to repeat that search from its start pad at the whole budget on every later call. The next
  call now first searches the same request from its goal for a tenth of the budget (at most 5 s). A* reports `no_path` only after expanding everything
  reachable, so when the goal sits in a small enclosed region the start-side search floods the open board and runs out of time while the goal-side one
  drains the small region: on a public 40 x 40 mm grid with a sealed 6 x 6 mm pocket, 602,924 expansions against 10,404. A drained probe ends the call
  with the timeout's own outcome: no route and no Python fallback. Any other probe outcome leaves the call as it was -- the start-side search keeps its
  whole budget and produces the same copper, and the probe's time is never taken from it. First attempts, unbounded searches, relief probes and a call
  whose budget exceeds every budget the search has already run out of are not probed. The timeout receipt reports `native_reverse_probes`,
  `native_reverse_proofs` and `native_reverse_seconds_saved`, and a per-call trace records each native search's `expansions`. To restore the old
  behaviour, set `KICAD_ROUTER_SKIP_REPEAT_FALLBACK=0`.
