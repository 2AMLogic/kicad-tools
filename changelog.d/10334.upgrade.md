- **Exported net keys of local labels in multi-sheet designs are now sheet-qualified** (Issue #10334). Code that stores or diffs net names from `extract_netlist(hierarchical=True)` / `build_netlist_from_schematic()` must update keys: a root-sheet label `I2C_SDA` is now `/I2C_SDA`, and a label `SHARED` in sheet `ChildWithLabels` is `/ChildWithLabels/SHARED` whether or not another sheet uses `SHARED`. Global labels and power nets (`GND`, `+3V3`) stay bare. Operating-state manifests for `ComponentStressAnalyzer` may keep the bare label.
