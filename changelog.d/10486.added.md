- **`kct route-link --join-track` ends a link on a track already connected to
  its destination pad** (Issue #10486). A second track entering a pad near a
  corner leaves slivers at the pad's mask opening that a fab's mask checker
  flags even though connectivity passes. The per-link opt-in routes to the
  nearest point of a straight track that is electrically connected to the
  named pad, kept half a trace width from every pad, and splits that track at
  the junction when the link is committed. A same-net track that only shares
  the name is not eligible. The per-link JSON reports the chosen `junction`
  (`x`, `y` in the board file's millimetres, and `layer`), and the junction
  honours the destination's `@layer` and `--link-layers`. Without the flag the
  exact-endpoint pad route is unchanged, for routes such as Kelvin sensing.
  When no such branch is routable the ordinary pad route is kept and
  `join_track_skipped` says why. A gate rejection restores the unsplit track
  with the branch.
