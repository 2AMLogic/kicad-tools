- **Starved-thermal remediation no longer silently skips its refill DRC on an inconclusive capability probe** (Issue #9946). `_kicad_drc_report(refill=True)` now passes `--refill-zones --save-board` unless the probe *definitively* said the flag is unsupported; a KiCad 8/9-style `Unknown argument: --refill-zones` rejection is retried once without the flags (and the definitive answer memoised), and a refill that could not run with the flags logs a WARNING instead of degrading to a read-only DRC in silence. The input net table stays snapshotted and restored around every launch.
