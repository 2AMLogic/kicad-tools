- **`kct export --stackup-id` can newly fail on filesystems without hard links** (Issue #10051). A named-stackup export whose board sits on exFAT/FAT removable media or some network mounts previously completed with a warning and shipped unrepaired silkscreen; it now exits 1 with nothing written. If you need the original artwork from such a location, pass `--no-silk-fix` explicitly, or move the board to a filesystem that supports hard links and export again.
