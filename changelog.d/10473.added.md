- **`kct export` preflight assesses soldered wire and ferrule landings without
  a component MPN** (Issue #10473). A `.kct` `wire_terminations` list declares
  each wire land by its exact KiCad reference and pad number, with the maximum
  outside diameter of the prepared end that enters the hole (`diameter_mm`: the
  tinned conductor at its widest, or the ferrule barrel) and the `source` of
  that measurement. A landing may be declared before it is measured; it then
  stays unassessed. The `assembly_lead_fit` check assesses a declared pad by its
  wire instead of its part's lead and reports the reference, pad, prepared
  diameter and source beside the usual nominal and minimum diametral margins,
  so a wire pad no longer needs a `bom_entries` assignment. Worked example: a
  2.40 mm prepared end in a 3.00 mm finished hole at -0.08 mm tolerance (2.92 mm
  minimum) clears by +0.60 mm nominal and +0.52 mm minimum; changing one pad to
  a 3.10 mm ferrule gives -0.10 mm and -0.18 mm, and only that pad fails. Wire
  declarations add no rows to the procurement BOM or the placement list.
