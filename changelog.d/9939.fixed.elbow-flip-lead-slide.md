- **`kct fix-drc --only mask-copper` slides a swapped pad-edge elbow out along the pad's lead when its entry would not be clean** (Issue #9939). The elbow swap is now held to the mask rule before anything moves: a swapped leg that overlaps the pad's opening has to be one clean entry, and one that misses it has to clear the opening by the manufacturer's minimum plus `--margin` (0.02 mm by default). When the entry leg fails that test and a pad lead continues from its far end, the repair slides the swapped corner out along the lead until both swapped legs clear the opening. The lead keeps its line and becomes the pad's only entry, every track angle is kept, and a slide that would split the net, crowd other-net copper, touch a locked track or crowd another opening is declined. A swap on a solder-mask-defined pad is declined as well, because the rule reports every entry there. On the committed Board 03 this clears the R4/2 opening that the swap had traded for a new finding: the public command converges at 4 mask errors instead of 5, with no new error identities and no off-angle tracks.
