- **A copper change that restores a failed net's full search budget now buys
  one full attempt, not a streak restart** (Issue #8486). When a foreign
  change touches the cells a failed search read, the next search still runs at
  the full per-net cap, but its no-progress failure re-anchors the back-off
  streak on the post-change copper while keeping its count -- the same
  contract the periodic full re-probe already had. Previously the count
  restarted at one, so a change was followed by a second, unearned
  full-budget search before the back-off resumed; on the reported board every
  iteration carried such a change. Seal-keyed witnesses, copper/seal regime
  switches and the deterministic (`cap is None`) arm still restart from one.
