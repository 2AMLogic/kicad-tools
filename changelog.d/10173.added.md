- **`kct check --mfr jlcpcb` warns on outer-layer tracks JLCDFM grades Danger** (Issue #10173).
  On the 4-layer 1 oz stackup, `F.Cu`/`B.Cu` tracks narrower than 0.1524 mm (6 mil) now
  report a `dimension_trace_width` **warning**: JLCDFM rated 0.1016 mm tracks Danger and
  0.1524 mm Good although JLCPCB's published 0.1016 mm floor allows them. The error floor is
  unchanged, inner layers and stackups without recorded evidence are not flagged, and the
  threshold is a new optional `trace_width` entry in the `dfm_thresholds` table (now v3).
  The Fab DFM parity table gains a `partial` `trace_width` row.
