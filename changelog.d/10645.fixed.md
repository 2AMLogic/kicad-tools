- **`kct check` no longer errors on a straight own-pad entry that is split exactly on the pad edge** (Issue #10645). The `within_own_pad` class granted its one-crossing allowance only to a segment with an authored end strictly inside the pad, so the outer piece of a track divided on the pad boundary read as an error while the identical unsplit entry, and the same split 0.2 mm further in, read as info. The outer piece is now read through its authored continuation -- the one other same-net straight segment at the joint, of the same width and layer, collinear, with no third segment there -- and gets the same allowance when that chain ends inside the pad, still measured on its own copper. An edge-ending piece with no continuation, a bend, a change of width or layer, a branch at the joint, a pass-through, different-net copper and any exposure beyond the pad keep their verdict.
