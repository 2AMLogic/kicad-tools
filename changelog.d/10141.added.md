- **`kct check` flags bypass capacitors far from their schematic-designated supply pin** (Issue #10141). New advisory `bypass_distance` category (warning; `--bypass-max-distance MM`, default 3.0) measures each cap against the pin chosen by the schematic cap-to-pin association instead of the nearest pad on the rail, so swapped caps on a shared rail are reported; caps with no defensible target get an info `bypass_distance_unassociated` finding, and a missing schematic is reported as not run. `kct detect-mistakes` "Bypass capacitor too far from power pin" now uses the same association (no second pairing) and reports itself incomplete when no schematic is found.
