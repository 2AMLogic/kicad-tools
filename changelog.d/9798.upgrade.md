- **Silk errors grade as errors under the jlcpcb profiles** (Issue #9798, operator ruling on #9795). `silk_over_copper` and `silkscreen_over_pad` (silk on exposed copper), `silkscreen_line_width` / `silkscreen_text_height` / `silkscreen_text_thickness` (below-minimum silk), and `silk_ref_obscured` / `silk_under_body` (silk hidden under a component body) now fail `kct check --mfr jlcpcb` / `jlcpcb-tier1` instead of warning. Every other profile keeps the legacy warning grading. Silk over a *via* is never an error: under these profiles an untented-via aperture hit grades as `info`, so a via-only board cannot fail through `silk_over_copper` either. The `silk_hole_overlap` severity split (PTH/NPTH error, via waived) is issue #9033 / PR #9112's change and lands separately. `kct route`'s post-route DRC gate defaults to `--mfr jlcpcb`, so a board whose silk text is below the minimum or sits on pads now exits non-zero after routing. The Board 06 and 07 recipes repair their silkscreen before final export. Their fixture gates reject any return of the 12 former Board 06 or 14 former Board 07 silk findings. Whether every committed `boards/*/output` artifact is free of these findings was not re-measured; historical fixtures with deliberately buried designators (the pre-#6394 board-03 witness) now show their `silk_ref_obscured` findings as errors.
