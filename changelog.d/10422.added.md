- **`kct export` measures each buttress-wire run against the Gerbers it ships** (Issue #10422). For a board with a `<board>.reinforcement.json` sidecar, the package's preflight report (`manifest.json`, `--format json`, `README.txt`) gains a `reinforcement_clearance` entry: for the joined mask strip on its own face and each anchor's opening on both faces, one edge-to-edge minimum to any other net's plotted copper (covered or not) and one to the part of it the same face's mask leaves exposed, each naming its nearest net, the pad when the X2 attributes identify one, and the copper and mask archive members with their SHA-256 digests. A strip that crosses foreign copper reports zero distance and positive exposure; edge contact is a zero gap with nothing exposed. The values are nominal geometry for engineering review with no verdict -- they never fail the export and leave `reinforcement_incomplete` and the creepage rule untouched. An opening the artwork cannot vouch for (a missing or mislabelled archive member, copper without an X2 net attribute, an anchor off its net's copper or off a plotted opening, two records claiming one strip) is reported incomplete with its reason, and valid results on the other face are kept.
