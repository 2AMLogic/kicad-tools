- **Preflight can now fail and warn on two-terminal SMD land patterns** (Issue #10505). When a `.kct` BOM entry declares a source-attributed `land_pattern` for an exact MPN, a confirmed copper geometry or pad-shape mismatch fails `assembly_smd_land_pattern` and blocks strict exports (`--strict-preflight`). Populated two-terminal SMD chips without a declared drawing for their exact MPN receive an *unassessed* warning naming each reference instead of passing silently.
