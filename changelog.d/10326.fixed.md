- **`kct check` silk-to-pad clearance follows footprint-level mask margins**
  (Issue #10326). `silk_pad_clearance` measured the mask aperture with the
  board margin whenever a pad carried no local override, so a footprint's
  `solder_mask_margin` was skipped: a real 0.065 mm gap went unreported, and a
  smaller footprint override of a larger board margin produced a false
  zero-clearance error. The aperture margin now resolves pad, then footprint,
  then board through the shared `pad_mask_margin` resolver.
