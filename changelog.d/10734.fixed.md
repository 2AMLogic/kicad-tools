- **`kct optimize-traces --pour-slots` keeps attached tracks on the 0/45/90/135 degree set** (Issue #10734). Spreading or packing a run no longer re-aims a 45-degree leg or straight continuation joined to its end. A lone diagonal keeps its line and the joint slides to the run's shifted line; anything else at the joint is left in place and the run is jogged back to it. An edit that could not stay on the angle grid is refused with `connected track would leave the angle grid` and leaves the copper untouched.
