- **`kct export` preflight checks a part's mounting holes against their drawing and the fab's finished-hole interval** (Issue #10476). A `.kct` `bom_entries` entry can declare the PC-board hole pattern a snap-in or similar package requires with `mounting_holes:` -- the two numbered pads (`pads: ['1', '2']`), the inclusive finished-hole `hole_diameter: {min_mm, max_mm}` and, when the drawing gives them, `pitch: {min_mm, max_mm}` -- plus a mandatory `source` citing the drawing; a range such as `C1-C2` applies it to each reference. `requirements.manufacturing.finished_hole_tolerance_upper_mm` (zero or positive, sharing `finished_hole_tolerance_source`) adds the upper side of the fabricator's tolerance beside the existing lower one, and `finished_hole_guarantees:` records an order-specific finished-hole interval the fab documented for selected `refs`. The new `assembly_mounting_hole_fit` check reports, per hole, the MPN, reference, pad, nominal drill, drawing limits, the applicable order interval with its source, and the verdict of the nominal drill and of each evidenced end of that interval; once per pair it reports the measured pitch. Limits are inclusive and judged before display rounding. A nominal mismatch is reported apart from a nominally correct hole whose order tolerance overruns a limit, naming the side and amount. A missing bound, another fab's order, a slot or a missing pad leaves that measurement unassessed and never passed. The declaration needs no `lead:` and leaves the `assembly_lead_fit` result unchanged.
