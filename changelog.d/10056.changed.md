- **Checked manufacturing rewrites keep a recoverable copy of the previous PCB** (Issue #10056). A successful verified rewrite (zone-fill refresh, silk legalisation) now leaves the displaced previous board in `.<board>.kicad_pcb.recovery-<timestamp>-<random>/original` beside the board, so a save landing through an editor's still-open handle after the export's re-check stays recoverable instead of being lost. One copy is kept per rewrite; `kct clean` lists and deletes them.
