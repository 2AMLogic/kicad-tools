- **`kct check` can newly report `sharp_trace_corner` on a trace corner within 0.001° below 1°** (Issue #10690). The sharp-corner window now opens at 0.999° instead of 1.000° and closes at 88.999° instead of 89.000°, so a deliberately drawn 0.9995° corner is flagged where it was treated as a collinear overlap, and a corner between 88.999° and 89° is no longer flagged. Re-check boards that carry trace corners within 0.001° of either edge.
