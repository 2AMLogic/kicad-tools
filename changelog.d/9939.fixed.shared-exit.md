- **`kct fix-drc --only mask-copper` can clear a shared pad exit** (Issue #9939). When moving a whole track would violate copper clearance, the repair can shorten overlapping outgoing tracks and slide their joined leads within the pad. It preserves the track angles and fixed endpoints, and checks the moved copper against project and manufacturer clearances. On Board 03 this clears three manufacturing errors at U1/18.
