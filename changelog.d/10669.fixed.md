- **A chamfered `roundrect` pad's cut corners are now missing from its copper outline** (Issue #10669). `pad_outline_is_exact()` required two or more selected corners, so a pad with a single `(chamfer ...)` corner was reported as exact and drawn as an ordinary, uncut `roundrect`: the strict connectivity check called a net complete where `kicad-cli pcb drc` reports an open. The shared pad outline now builds every selected corner as its straight cut and keeps the authored rounding on the others (a pad with two or more cut corners gets the same outline instead of an eroded size box), so strict connectivity reports what KiCad does.
