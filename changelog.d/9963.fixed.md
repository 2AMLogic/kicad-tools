- **`kct route` under-detects progress on pad-escape boards** (Issue #9963). The known-failure progress test for a partially connected net measured connectivity over the raw physical pads, but the negotiated search routes against escape endpoints (`_escape_pad_overrides`), and the escape stub copper never enters the net's route list — so a re-search that genuinely gained a terminal at an escape endpoint read as no-progress and kept the net's failure streak and search back-off. Progress is now measured over the routing-terminal pads (escape-substituted), matching the loop's own `pads_by_net` view. A no-progress re-search whose entry copper can no longer be re-landed legally also ratchets the failure entry to the kept result, so a second search of the net in the same iteration cannot re-land the superseded entry copper beside it.
