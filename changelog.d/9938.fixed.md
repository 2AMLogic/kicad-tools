- **`kct zones fill` no longer reports success with an empty copper pour after a KiCad capability probe times out** (Issue #9938). When the `kicad-cli pcb drc --help` probe was killed, timed out or empty, the fill dropped both `--refill-zones` and `--save-board`; on KiCad 10 the plain DRC then wrote a report, saved nothing, and the command printed `Zones filled` over a board byte-identical to its unfilled input (10.376 mm² of F.Cu instead of 255.033 mm² on a 20 × 20 mm control, with zero DRC errors). An inconclusive probe now requests the explicit refill and save and lets kicad-cli's own parser decide; a definitive `Unknown argument: --refill-zones` rejection restores the original copper and retries once without the flags. Every flag-driven run must end in a verified on-disk save, so a report written over an unchanged board is an explicit failure, and a failed fill leaves a pre-existing `--output` file as the caller had it. The post-fill thermal remediation now skips its first refill only when the completed fill invocation verifiably saved copper, never on the strength of a later help reply.
