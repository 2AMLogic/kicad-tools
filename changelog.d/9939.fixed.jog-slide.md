- **`kct fix-drc --only mask-copper` can slide a jog out of a pad opening** (Issue #9939). When a diagonal track between two parallel runs enters a same-net pad opening, the repair translates the jog (and any coincident copies) along those runs instead of recentring its end. Every track keeps its angle, the far ends of both runs stay fixed, and the move is declined if it would split the net's copper, crowd other-net copper or leave a run too short. On the committed Board 03 the public command now converges at 25 mask errors (was 35 after #10068), with no new error identities and no off-angle tracks.
