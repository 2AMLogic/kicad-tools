- **`kct fix-drc --only mask-copper` can swap the legs of an elbow that hugs a pad edge** (Issue #9939). An overlapping pass-through track that runs along a pad's edge and turns in at its corner is never an own-pad entry. When it and exactly one other same-net track form a two-leg chain between two fixed anchors, the repair now takes the mirror elbow so the corner lands strictly inside the pad. Both legs keep their length and angle, only the shared corner moves, and the move is declined if it would split the net's copper, crowd other-net copper, touch a locked or generated track, or leave the corner outside the pad. A via that reaches a track an earlier repair already moved now waits for the next pass instead of editing it twice and rolling both back. On the committed Board 03 the public command now converges at 18 mask errors (was 25), with no new error identities, no off-angle tracks, and zero native violations or opens on saved and refilled copper.
