- **A purchasing terminal map can no longer land on a footprint its row does not
  list** (Issue #10582). When a `purchasing` row declares `refs`, every mapped
  `terminals[].ref` must be one of them. Previously a `ref` typo that named
  another real footprint with a matching pad and net passed the PCB check and
  was frozen into `purchasing.json` under the wrong part. The spec error names
  the row, terminal, offending reference and declared references, and `kct export`
  reports it before publishing the list or replacing a previous bundle.
