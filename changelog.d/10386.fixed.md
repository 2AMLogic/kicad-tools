- **Readiness evidence says why LCSC stock went unverified** (Issue #10386). The
  `bom_lcsc_stock` preflight recorded the same "Stock not verified" warning whether
  the generating host had no offline catalog, had one it could not read, or had
  searched one that does not list the part, so a bundle regenerated on a worker
  without `kct parts sync-catalog` read like a finding about the board. The check now
  records a `sources` block (`parts_cache`, `offline_catalog`) naming the state each
  lookup reached: `hit`, `miss`, `missing`, `unreadable` or `not_consulted`. It
  travels from `manifest.json` into `metrics.lcsc_stock_order` in `readiness.json`
  and a "parts data sources" line in the manufacturing `README.txt`, the unverified
  warning names the condition, and `--verify` keeps the producer's record rather
  than the verifying host's. Verdicts and severities are unchanged, and a bundle
  recorded without the block verifies as before and claims nothing about sources.
