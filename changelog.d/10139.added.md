- **`kct check` audits net-class agreement between sidecar, project and copper** (Issue #10139). New `netclass_agreement` category (rule ids `netclass_sidecar_mismatch`, `netclass_track_width_mismatch`), on by default; findings are advisory warnings, waivable through `.kct_waivers.json`. It reports a routed net whose class or `trace_width` in `<stem>.net_class_map.json` differs from what the project's `net_settings` resolves, and a net whose copper is narrower than its resolved class `track_width`. Silent when there is no sidecar, no `.kicad_pro`, or no non-`Default` class.
