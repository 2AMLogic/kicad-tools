- **A nominal 89° trace corner gets the same verdict from `kct check` and the router on every platform** (Issue #10677). The `sharp_trace_corner` rule, the `explain` acid-trap check and the router's fold-back gate now share one angle-window predicate whose 89° upper edge carries a 1e-9° float-rounding guard. Before, the computed angle of a nominal 89° corner fell a few 1e-13° either side of the threshold depending on the board position and the platform `libm` (88.99999999999999° on macOS, 89.0° on Linux), so it was flagged as an acid trap in some places and not others. The 1° lower edge and the manufacturing thresholds are unchanged.
