- **`kct check` flags silk inside a keepout that forbids it, and `SilkscreenRepairer` can clip it out** (Issue #9798). A rule area whose sibling `.kicad_dru` carries an area-specific `(constraint disallow text graphic)` on a silk layer — the only way KiCad can forbid printed ink in a keepout, none of the five built-in flags covers it — now produces one `silk_in_keepout` **error** per (silk item, area): the front line crossing the area and the front text inside it are flagged, the back line at the same coordinates is not, and an area with no such rule (a plain copper keepout, however strict) leaves the silk legal. `repair_keepout_clearance` clips `fp_`/`gr_` line/rect/poly silk out of those areas with the same stroke-width clearance the check measures, keeps both printable portions of a crossing line, is idempotent, and reports surviving text as `<item> in <area>` residuals that stay errors — the clip operation the #9797 legalisation pass consumes.
- **`kct check` flags silk hidden under another component's body** (Issue #9798). `silk_ref_obscured` covered references against their own footprint only; the new `silk_under_body` rule extends the operator's "silk under footprints" to any silk element (graphics or text, footprint-owned or board-level) lying entirely inside *another* footprint's courtyard on the same side. A silk item merely crossing a courtyard is not reported.
- **`kct fix-silkscreen --all` clips silk out of keepouts, and keepout rules keep their meaning** (Issue #9798). `legalize_silk` now runs `repair_keepout_clearance` against the board's sibling `.kicad_dru`, lists `silk_in_keepout` in `rules_covered`, reports clipped graphics (`keepout_clip_fixes`, with the real layer) and leaves text in a keepout as an unresolved item; graphics still in a keepout make the command exit 1. Each rule's side/kind pairing is kept per area (front-text and back-graphic rules on one area no longer forbid all four combinations), and a condition other than `A.intersectsArea('…')` terms joined by `||` — a negation, say — is reported as `silk_keepout_rule_unsupported` and not applied, instead of being read as a positive area match. The check and repair honor ignored rules, including higher-priority exceptions, and find a disallow constraint anywhere in its rule.
