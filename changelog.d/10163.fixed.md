- **`kct readiness` ignores renders under a declared output root of any name** (Issue #10163). With `[readiness] output_root = "release"` (or `out`, `build/release`, ...), files under `<output_root>/renders/`, `<output_root>/manufacturing/renders/` and `<output_dir>/renders/` are no longer hashed into `readiness.json` `inputs`, so re-rendering them no longer invalidates the committed receipt. Board-local `output/renders/` exclusions are unchanged.
