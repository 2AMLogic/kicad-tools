- **Schematic net identity is now one answer across `get_net_for_pin`, `get_all_pin_nets` and `extract_netlist`** (Issue #10240). A rail carrying two names (e.g. two `VDD_LOCAL` labels plus a `+3V3` power symbol) used to resolve to `+3V3` for one pin and `VDD_LOCAL` for another, and `extract_netlist` picked the label over the power symbol. Same-named labels on separate wire branches now join, and the winning name follows KiCad's priority (global label, power symbol, local label, hierarchical label). Board LVS builds on this identity, so expected net names can change for schematics that name one rail twice.
