- **`kct export` rewrites the source board's zone fills, and can stop when it cannot** (Issue #9987). When export changes the DRC rules of a board with saved zone copper, it refills the zones in place before plotting and reports `Export refilled the zone fills in <board> to match the generated DRC rules.` among the preflight warnings. When that refill cannot be produced -- no `kicad-cli`, a kicad-cli that rejects `--refill-zones --save-board` (KiCad 8/9), a killed or crashed run, or a report written without a save -- the export stops with `Nothing was exported`, restores the board and leaves the new rules in place: refill the zones in KiCad and export again. A `drc_report.json` beside the board that was bound to the pre-refill bytes is no longer accepted as passing evidence, because it describes older copper; re-run `kct check --output` after the first such export.
