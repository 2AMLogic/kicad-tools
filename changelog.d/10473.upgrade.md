- **A declared wire or ferrule can now fail preflight and block strict exports**
  (Issue #10473). Once a `wire_terminations` declaration carries a verified
  prepared `diameter_mm` and its `source`, a prepared end that cannot clear the
  finished plated hole -- nominally, or at the fabricator's declared lower
  finished-hole tolerance -- fails `assembly_lead_fit`, and `kct export
  --strict-preflight` refuses the package. A declared pad without a diameter or
  source stays *unassessed* (a warning naming the pad), as before for pads with
  no verified lead dimensions.
