- **`pour_slot` no longer reports packed parallel tracks** (Issue #10121). A pair whose edge-to-edge gap is at or below `2 x zone clearance` never had a fillable strip between the clearance keep-outs, so `min_thickness` had no island to drop: its copper-free channel is the unavoidable clearance void of any two different-net conductors at that spacing, not a pour slot. Measured on Board 05's nine remaining advisories against the native KiCad 10.0.6 copper film (all packed at 0.250/0.275/0.395 mm against a 0.400 mm packed edge, blind channels sealed by pad copper and escape via lands, front pour one connected region) and on two native-refill controls: a 0.55 mm dropped-island slot still warns, a 0.395 mm pad-exit pair clears. Genuine findings in the dropped-island band (`2 x clearance < gap < 2 x clearance + min_thickness`) are unchanged, and the criterion now matches the `--pour-slots` repair pass's own packed-pair cutoff.
