- **Negotiated re-routes of failed nets keep their reduced search budget unless
  copper the failed search actually examined changed** (Issue #8486). The
  native pathfinder now records the grid cells a failed per-net search read; a
  foreign shape that was present at the first failure and has since moved away
  only restores the full per-net budget when its clearance envelope reaches
  those cells, instead of whenever it sat inside the coarse reachable-space
  scope. Searches that fell back to the Python A* record nothing and keep the
  previous rule, as do the periodic full re-probe, the seal-keyed back-off and
  the deterministic (`cap is None`) arm. Private 97-net board timings are not
  yet measured; see the issue.
