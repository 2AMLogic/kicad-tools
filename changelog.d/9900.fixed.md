- **`kct route` reports why a targeted rip-up rolled back** (Issue #9900). `victim_budget` identifies an expired deadline or an exhausted expansion budget on a victim edge. `victim_geometric` requires a drained search on every failed edge, including the Python fallback when the C++ router is active. A rejected copper commit reports `victim_commit_refused`. Other failures without a definitive cause report `victim_unproven`. The displaced nets are restored on rollback.
