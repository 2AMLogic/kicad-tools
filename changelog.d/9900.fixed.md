- **`kct route` no longer reports a budget-stopped victim as a geometric seal** (Issue #9900). A targeted rip-up whose victim search stopped at its deterministic node-expansion cap (the Board 06 seed-42 pure-Python run's USB3_RX1/TX1 victims, which the C++ backend routes on the same board) is now recorded and logged as `victim_budget` instead of `victim_geometric`; the geometric reason is reserved for searches that drained their open set. The `victim_budget` note also appears in the pure-Python backend status, naming the intentional search-capacity limitation.
