- **`kct creepage --pair NET_A NET_B` measures only the named net pairs** (Issue #10342).
  Repeatable and unordered; neither net needs to be HV, and with `--voltage-map` an
  endpoint absent from the map is reported instead of defaulting to 0 V. Reuses the
  census's geometry, rules and witnesses, skips board-edge rows and reports passing
  cross-face paths. Table and JSON (`diagnostic`, `unresolved_pairs`, per-row
  `governing_voltage_v`) are marked as a limited diagnostic; unknown nets,
  unmeasurable pairs and violations exit non-zero. Without `--pair` the command,
  schema and exit codes are unchanged; audit and readiness still use the full census.
