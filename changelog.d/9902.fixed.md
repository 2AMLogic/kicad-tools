- **Via relocation no longer blocked board-wide by slotted or offset foreign pads** (Issue #9902). The shared hole-floor checks behind `kct fix-vias --relocate-in-pad`, the drill-clearance slide gate and the copper-gap via repair failed closed on any foreign pad whose drill they could not model -- a slot, a drill offset or a `custom` pad -- with no distance test, so one USB-C shield slot vetoed every via move on the board however far away (Board 03: a via 41.662 mm from J1 was refused). Slot drills are now modelled as their rotated stadium at the pad anchor and offset drills as discs with the offset displacing the copper (matching KiCad's own placement, pinned against the KiCad 10 image's drill and gerber exports), so verdicts are real distance tests; `custom` pads use their exact anchor-plus-primitives outline. Genuinely unprovable geometry (malformed drills, refused custom primitives) still fails closed with the same named obstruction.
